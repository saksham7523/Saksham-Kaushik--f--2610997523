World Map Explorer

Click on a country on the map below to open an information page in a new tab.

How the shapes work

  • rect (USA, Egypt): coords are left,top,right,bottom.
  • circle (Brazil, Japan): coords are center-x,center-y,radius.
  • poly (India, Australia): coords are a list of x,y points that outline the shape.
  • default: any click on the image that doesn't land in another area (the blue ocean, for example) opens the World Map page.
